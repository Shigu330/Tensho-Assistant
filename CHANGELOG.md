### English
- Garden (reliability): the CI of 0.1405 failed once (1 of the last 60 runs) on « Garden journal: a detected planting replaces the bed… »: a garden regular expression ran past its 50 ms timeout while the tests ran in parallel (RegexMatchTimeoutException). The garden's expressions (a bed's place, the planting chat line, the bed menu's title) now share a one-second timeout (GardenJournal.MatchTimeout; their inputs are bounded to 40, 200 and 80 characters); a timeout reads as « nothing read » instead of leaving the page's drawing or the chat reading.
