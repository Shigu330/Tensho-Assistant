### English
- The chocobo companion's reminder: no more false « has left » after sending it away and then teleporting or mounting (X-777, finding I1 of the review of 0.1784). The first goings and comings after each load of the plugin (6 lines) are written to the log with the game's timer, to see in the game whether it is kept during the absence. Evidence 0.1780 completed.
- For players: Chocobo companion reminder: no false « has left » any more after sending it away and then teleporting or mounting.
