## 0.1499.0

- Minor findings of the 0.1472 review (X-492). Un fichier qui tient exactement ses bornes n'est plus réécrit à chaque ouverture.

### English
- Chat history on this PC: « No limit » for the messages and for the days (X-513, the user's request of 11/16 around 11:10 am, choice (a)). Beside « Messages kept » and « Days kept », a « No limit » box each; the number is greyed while it is ticked and kept for when it is unticked. Days by default: 30 instead of 7 (messages by default unchanged, 2,000); a configuration already saved keeps its values. The place the character's history takes on disk is shown under the settings (measured off the game's thread after each read, write, trim and erase; in KB, MB or GB). The consent question says « no limit » in words. « Erase the history » stays. The trim goes through the whole file line by line (never held whole in memory) instead of writing back only what had been read: nothing is lost past the part read back, and nothing is removed for a « No limit » bound. A file that holds exactly its bounds is no longer written again at each open.
- For players: Chat history: « No limit » boxes for the messages and the days kept, 30 days by default, and the place it takes on disk shown; a big history still loads at once.
