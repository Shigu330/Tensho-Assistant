- No « harvested » notice of Tensho followed (the journal's chat notices are in the same log as `kind 1` lines): the harvest rule of 0.978
  (a type 62 line while the target is a garden bed, `docs/evidence/0.978-garden-harvest.md`) did not match; the target is not read in the log.
- The bed's menu was read 1.3 to 1.6 s before each gain line. Rule kept (0.1360): in housing, a type 62 line without a sender is a harvest when
  the target is a garden bed, or when a bed's menu was read at the same place within 10 s; that bed is the one harvested, each menu counts once,
  and the other gain lines of the same harvest (crop, seeds) count once (5 s, 0.997). In developer mode the harvest is logged with the target
  (« Garden harvest read: patch …, bed …, target … ») to see what the target was.
- The items of a harvest are handed to the item sources as `GainSource.Garden` (the bed's number).

## X-383: crossbreeds after a replanting

Two screenshots of the user: beds 2, 4, 6, 8 (carrots) removed by hand then planted again (2 d 23 h left); the forecasts of beds 3, 5, 7
(apples, 1 d 20 h left) became « no crossbreed ». The forecast was read from the journal of now, skipping a neighbour planted after the bed.

- Rule (Console Games Wiki, `docs/evidence/0.456-garden-grid.md`): the crossbreed is decided when the seed is planted, with a neighbour
  already there. Since 0.1360 each bed keeps the neighbours planted at its planting (`GardenBed.NeighboursAtPlanting`) and its forecast reads
  them, whatever happens to the neighbours afterwards.
- Bed 1 (the user's journal: planted 2026-11-11 19:29:38 UTC, the first of the patch, before the carrots): never a crossbreed, by the wiki's
  rule (the first seed of an empty patch has no neighbour).
- Beds noted before 0.1360 kept nothing: when a neighbour is empty or was planted after them, the partner may have been removed since:
  « not known », never « no crossbreed » (R06).
