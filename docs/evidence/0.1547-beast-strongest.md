# Beastmaster labyrinth: the strongest enemy of a fight (0.1547, X-579)

## The request

The user's capture of 18/11 around 00:25 (0.1546, the 1st board's Guardian): the call order gave 1. Écureuil (Fire, both succubi), 2. Pampa
and 3. Cu sith (Piercing, Pas de Seul). The user: « careful, it takes every monster, even those the boss summons, while the weaknesses must be
of the strongest monster first ».

## The sheets

`tools/XbmProbe "<sqpack>" French XBMBattleDetail XBMContentBattle` (18/11, game 2026.11.03.0000.0000). `XBMContentBattle` 1.0 = 6: the 1st
board's Guardian is `XBMBattleDetail` 6:

| Subrow | Name (BNpcName) | Stars (Str, Int, Phys. res., Mag. res., Vit.) | Sum | Weakness (XBMElement) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.0 | 14541 (Pas de Seul) | 2, 5, 3, 5, 5 | 20 | 8 (Piercing) |
| 6.1 | 14542 (succubus) | 1, 2, 1, 1, 2 | 7 | 1 (Fire) |
| 6.2 | 14543 (succubus) | 2, 1, 1, 1, 2 | 7 | 1 (Fire) |

- No column marks an enemy as summoned or as the fight's leader: column 0 the name, 1 to 5 the stars, 6 and 7 the actions, 8 the weakness,
  10 the icon; column 9 (247, 248, 2, 210…) is not read and its meaning is unknown.
- Subrow 0 is the strongest (or tied) in most fights of more than one enemy, but not all: row 12 (5 against 10 and 7), row 42 (9 against 14).
  So the order in the sheet does not say who is strongest; the stars do.
- The sheet gives no HP: the Vitality star stands for them (DescriptionString 1751: Vitalité, maximum HP).

## What the plugin does

- `BeastTeams.Power`: an enemy's strength is the sum of its five stars. `BeastTeams.Weights`: a stronger enemy weighs more than all the weaker
  ones together (enemies of the same strength weigh the same; all as strong: 1 each, the count of 0.1544).
- A fight's three (`BeastTeams.Best`) and their calling order (`CallOrder`) count the enemies hit by their weight: at the Guardian, a Piercing
  beast (Pas de Seul) is called first, then the Fire one. The « weakness hit n of m » count is unchanged.
- The reason of each beast names the enemies it aims at, the strongest first, marked « (the strongest) » (`BeastUi.Strongest`).
- The 10 to take are chosen as before (the weaknesses of the most enemies over the board); plans are made again (`PlanVersion` 4).
