# Fishing sources (GatherBuddy, Garland and Teamcraft): what the game can teach Tensho

## 1. Where Tensho reads these sources, and which fields it uses

- **GatherBuddy** (`Community/GatherBuddyFish.cs:15-17`, parsed at `:66-90`) gives each fish these fields: Baits, Mooches, HookSet, Bite, Start/EndMinute (ET window), Weathers, Transitions (previous weather), Lure, Predators (fish and count), Snag, SpearSize/SpearSpeed and Intuition. `Route()` (`:33-45`) builds the bait and mooch chain. They are used in these places:
  - Fishing panel row (`MainWindow.FishingPanel.cs:173-192`): route, bite and hookset, conditions, lure, snag, predators, intuition.
  - "Now" marks (`:36-52`): window, weather, previous weather.
  - Gathering page (`MainWindow.GatheringFish.cs:31-58`).
  - Fish windows and alarms (`Gathering/FishWindows.cs:19-55`, `MainWindow.Alarms.cs:131-136`).
  - Ocean voyage bait and bite (`MainWindow.OceanVoyagePanel.cs:39-42`).
  - Spearfishing candidates (`Gathering/Spearfishing.cs:28`, `MainWindow.SpearfishingPanel.cs:35`).
- **Garland** (`Community/FishingSources.cs:12-46`, `FishingConditions.cs:12-31`) gives spot ↔ fish, bait chains, ET hours and weather names. It is only the fallback (`FishingPanel.cs:194-202`, `MainWindow.FishingSources.cs`).
- **Teamcraft** (`Community/FishBiteTimes.cs:96-106`) gives bite seconds per fish and spot, summarised as Min/Max/Usual/Reports (`:17-29`). The panel shows it against the cast stopwatch (`FishingPanel.cs:135-142`).

## 3. Proposed parsers

All of them would live in one `FishingObserver`:
- It runs on the framework thread, reads every 100 ms only while ConditionFlag.Fishing is set, copies the values and keeps them bounded. It is cut in PvP (CompetitiveGate) and sends nothing anywhere.
- Places are the FishingSpot sheet's own X/Z, never the player's position, so `DiscoveryStore.Valid` (`DiscoveriesModel.cs:148-151`) still holds.
- The fish is attributed only when exactly one fish item comes in within 3 s of the hook.

| Kind | Subject | Detail | Territory, Map, cell | Levels | When it records | Feature that prefers it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FishCatch=4` | fish item | first bait item, or the mooched fish | the spot's | bite type (1 !, 2 !!, 3 !!!, 0 unknown) | after a validated catch. If the fish is not in `FishingSpot.Item`, nothing is recorded. | `Route()` and the bite column take the discovered route and bite for this spot first, otherwise GatherBuddy, otherwise Garland, and the source is shown in the tooltip. |
| `FishBiteTime=5` | fish item | bait | the spot's | bite seconds (Min, Max). The 0–200 bound has to be widened, because Teamcraft allows up to 600 s. | Bite − cast. Recorded only with no Chum or Patience; those casts get their own flag. | The bite column uses own data at 5 catches or more (Seen ≥ 5), otherwise Teamcraft. A histogram field is needed for the usual second. |
| `FishConditionSeen=6` | fish item | weather id, plus the previous weather in a new field | the spot's | ET minute of the catch (min, max) | after each catch | "Now" and the windows show "seen in…". The sheet's TimeRestriction/WeatherRestriction set to 0 overrides any community window. |
| `FishIntuition=7` | intuition fish | predator item | the spot's | count before the status started; length in seconds in a new field | when Status 568 appears | `FishingPanel.cs:186-190` |
| `FishLure=8` | fish item | AmbitiousLure 1 / ModestLure 2 | the spot's | — | after a catch on a lure cast | `FishingPanel.cs:184` |
| `SpearfishSeen=9` | fish item | — | the point's | size and speed | only with a single fish on screen | `Spearfishing.Candidates` |

`DiscoveryFile.CurrentVersion` goes up to 2. Import still goes through `Valid`, with ranges per kind.

## 4. Ranking (value, then effort)

1. **Fishing log flags (no data to collect).** The flags are already copied: show "no time or weather condition" from the sheet. This removes the community data for every unrestricted fish. Effort: S.
2. **`FishCatch`** (route and mooch per spot). It replaces the largest part of the GatherBuddy and Garland bait data. Effort: M (handler id probe, inventory event).
3. **`FishBiteTime`.** It replaces Teamcraft entirely for the spots you fish. Effort: M (needs a histogram field and a 600 s bound).
4. **`FishConditionSeen`.** Partial value, because it shows what was seen and never a requirement. Effort: S once 2 exists.
5. **Bite type** inside `FishCatch`. High value, but it is blocked until the probe proves where the game carries it. Effort: M.
6. **`FishIntuition` and `FishLure`.** Few fish, but these are exactly the hard ones. Effort: S–M.
7. **`SpearfishSeen`.** Low value, ambiguous attribution. Effort: M.

The hookset and any ET or weather requirement stay with GatherBuddy until they can be proved in game; the report shows them as "unknown", never invented.

Nothing in the repository was edited; the only commands run were ApiProbe checks of the types named above.

**3. Proposed parsers (new values for `DiscoveryKind` in `Discoveries/DiscoveriesModel.cs:9`)**

Place cells apply only to drops. All other kinds leave Territory and the cell at 0, so the `Key` (:33) becomes item:source.

- **ItemFromItem (4)**: Subject = item obtained, Detail = source item, LevelMin = kind (same numbering as ItemSupplement), LevelMax = the largest quantity seen.
  - Trigger: the SalvageResult or PurifyResult agent result; otherwise, inside one framework tick, exactly one stack loses one unit and exactly one item is added, while no shop, trade or retainer addon is open.
  - Check: the source item has the ItemAction or desynthesis flag in the Item sheet. Desynthesis and reduction count after one sighting; a coffer counts after two.
- **DutyLoot (5)**: Subject = item, Detail = CFC row, Territory = the duty's territory, LevelMin = chest EObj BaseId (0 = completion reward).
  - Trigger: a new `LootItem` shows up in Loot, or ItemAdded within 10 seconds after DutyCompleted.
  - Check: CFC is not 0 and the zone is not PvP (CompetitiveZones). No player data is kept, so roll results are ignored.
- **MobDrop (6)**: Subject = item, Detail = BNpcName, Territory, Map and the 50-yalm cell, LevelMin/LevelMax = the monster's levels.
  - Trigger: ItemAdded within 3 seconds after the player's own target (a BattleNpc, never a player) reaches 0 HP.
  - Check: not in a duty, not in PvP, no quest event condition, no other inventory change in that window. The fact counts only after two sightings.
  - This is PvE information only, about monsters; nothing helps in combat.
- **VentureReward (7)**: Subject = item, Detail = RetainerTask row, LevelMax = quantity. Trigger: the venture result opens and the agent is copied. No retainer name is kept.
- **FateReward (8)**: Subject = item, Detail = Fate row. Trigger: ItemAdded within 15 seconds after the current FATE ends. Counts after two sightings.
- **ShopNpcPlace (9)**: Subject = ENpc, Detail = shop id, plus the place. Trigger: a shop addon opens with an ENpc as target.

**How features prefer discovered data**

- Each Lumina or Garland accessor gets a discovered layer in front. For example, `MonstersOf` = discovered MobDrop facts, then the CSV rows that are not discovered yet.
- The hover shows "seen ×N" next to discovered data and keeps the community credit only for the rest.
- A Discoveries page counts per kind "discovered / community / overlap". When the overlap reaches a set level, the user can turn the community source off (the switch already exists in Settings › Advanced).

**4. Ranking (value, then effort)**

| # | Parser | Replaces | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ItemFromItem | ItemSupplement (19 kinds, the largest CSV) | medium; desynthesis is low |
| 2 | DutyLoot | 4 Dungeon CSVs and Garland instance documents | medium |
| 3 | MobDrop | MobDrop CSV, Garland mob sources, MonsterLootService | high: knowing which kill gave the item is the risk |
| 4 | VentureReward | RetainerVentureItem | low |

| 6 | ShopNpcPlace | ENpcShop/ENpcPlace, only the NPCs the sheets do not place | low |

These remain community data: StoreItem, the fishing bait routes, and QuestRequiredItem until probed.

Common rules for all parsers:
- Native data is read on the framework thread and copied right away.
- At most 16 inventory events are kept per tick.
- Everything goes to the local `Discoveries.json` only.
- A settings switch for each kind, like those at `MainWindow.Discoveries.cs:104-107`.

**Universalis and the other community sources: what Tensho can learn in game (read-only analysis, no files changed)**

Visited worlds are recorded today, but only the current world's quote ever uses them. Nothing else in Tensho reads prices seen on other worlds.

### 1. What Tensho takes from Universalis, and the in-game verdict

| Feature (file:line) | Universalis facts used | In-game verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Hover, craft lists, profit, currency value (`MarketService.cs:90,265`; `MarketQuote.cs:72-79`) | Lowest offer NQ/HQ, last sale and its date, average sale, sales a day; for the world being looked up | Observable: `IMarketBoard.OfferingsReceived` / `HistoryReceived` when the board is opened (already read, `SeenPriceReader.Dalamud.cs:162`) |
| Same (`MarketQuote.cs:67-68`, hover `MainWindow.MarketHover.cs:418-429`, `PreparationCost.cs:100`, `CraftProfit.cs:16`) | Cheapest offer on the data center and on the region, with its world and upload time | Observable only for worlds the player visits (the board shows the current world only). Worlds never visited cannot be observed. |
| Sales speed over 7 days (`MarketQuote.cs:31-46`) | 200 history entries | Partly: the game gives the last 20 sales per opening. Kept and merged across openings, they grow longer. |
| Market page board (`MarketBoardService.cs`) | Offers and sales by world / data center / region; the data-center to region table (`/data-centers`) | Offers: observable per visited world. The region table is game data: sheet `WorldDCGroupType.Region` (checked with ApiProbe). |
| Retainer comparison (`RetainerSales.cs:106`, on click) | Offers of the home world | Observable when the player opens the board at home |

**Are visited worlds recorded today?** Yes. The reader stores under `market.State.WorldId` (`Plugin.cs:187`), and that is `player.CurrentWorld` (`MarketWorldReader.Dalamud.cs:41`). So offers seen on a visited world go into `MarketSeen.json` under that world. What is missing:

- **Never used back home.** `GetCached(state.WorldId,…)` (`MarketService.cs:170-175`, hover `:403`) only looks at the current world. The data-center and region cheapest offers come from Universalis only (`SeenPrices.cs:48-49`).
- **No age limit.** A seen quote wins whenever it is newer than Universalis (`SeenPrices.cs:60`). With Universalis off it stands forever, whatever its age.
- **Waits for Universalis.** While Universalis is on and has not answered, the seen quote is held back (`Plugin.cs:185`, `SeenPrices.cs:59`).
- **Sales history replaced, not merged** (`SeenPrices.cs:93`), so speed never grows past one window of 20 sales.
- **Full store drops new entries.** At 50,000 entries, new ones are refused instead of evicting the oldest (`SeenPrices.cs:101`). More worlds fill it faster.
- **Upload times thrown away.** Universalis gives an upload time per world, but only the current world's and the cheapest world's are kept (`MarketQuote.cs:63-68`). A per-world "which is newer" comparison is therefore impossible.
- **Market page board ignores seen prices entirely.**

### 2. Proposals for Universalis, best first

**P1: prefer seen prices on every world (high value, small effort).** Keep the store in `SeenPriceStore` rather than as a `DiscoveryFact`.
- Add `SeenPriceStore.Cheapest(dcWorlds, hq, now, maxAge)`: the lowest seen offer per world, newest first.
- In `SeenPrices.Quote`, the data-center cheapest becomes the minimum over:
  - each data-center world's seen entry, when it is newer than that world's Universalis upload time;
  - Universalis' cheapest, only when its world has no newer seen entry.
- Keep the full `worldUploadTimes` dictionary in `MarketQuote` for this comparison.
- Region cheapest: the same over the region's worlds, taken from `World` + `WorldDCGroupType.Region`.
- Validity proposal:
  - offers count as "current" for 24 h (the same 24 h as `MarketQuote.Recent`, `:51-52`), then show as "seen N days ago";
  - sales are kept for 30 days;
  - entries older than 90 days are purged.
- With Universalis off, or its offers older than the seen ones, the seen quote stands alone and its age is shown.

**P2: merge sales histories (medium value, small effort).** `SeeSales` adds sales instead of replacing them, removing duplicates by (time, price, quantity, HQ). Keep up to 200 sales or 30 days. The 7-day speed is then computed locally once the history covers 7 days; before that it stays "unknown", not zero.

**P3: region table from game data (small value, tiny effort).** Replace `/data-centers` with `WorldDCGroupType.Region` and `World.DataCenter`.

**P4: Market page board from what was seen (medium value, medium effort).** For the world scope, build the board from the seen entries (offers, sales, age) when they are newer. For data-center and region scopes, merge seen worlds and Universalis per world. No retainer names: the seen store never keeps them.

**P5: retainer comparison from seen home-world offers (medium value, medium effort).** When the offers are read, mark which ones belong to the player's own retainers (`IMarketBoardItemListing.RetainerId` compared with the own retainer ids). Only the yes/no flag is stored, no id. The competitor's name stays unknown without Universalis; say so in the UI.
