# 0.1604: retainers' market sales missing from the gil journal (X-658)

The user's report of 20/11 (a capture of Storage › Gil, 7 days, kept out of the repository): « I received gil from the market and it
wasn't counted ». X-662 M3 (0.1606, review of 0.1605): the amounts and times below are invented, of the same shape as the user's journal
and with the same relations between them; the user's own balances and times stay out of the public repository. On the capture (in these
figures): Retainers, Market +124,507; Retainers, transfers, spent 324,389; Character, transfers, gained 320,834.

The withdrawal at T + 94 s left a balance of 198,760 that no entry ever raised: those 198,760 gil of sales were counted nowhere. And
120,340 + 612 + 3,555 = 124,507 (the « Market » of the capture); 120,340 + 198,760 + 1,734 + 3,555 = 324,389 (the retainers' transfers:
both withdrawals, the two of D−3 and the falling half of each pair); 120,340 + 198,760 + 1,122 + 612 = 320,834 (the character's). The gap
of 3,555 = 437 + 1,122 + 1,122 + 437 + 437: the pairs one second apart, a fall then a rise of the same amount (or the reverse), each
counted as a transfer and as a sale.

## Causes, from the code and dalamud.log of 20/11

1. `dalamud.log`: « Earlier instance 0.1600.0 loaded at … », 19 s before the withdrawal at T + 94 s: the plugin reloaded (a relay's new
   version) while the second retainer was open (« Reading retainer », the sale list read, 37 s before the load). `GilWatcher` took its
   first reading after the load as a starting point, never a change (by design since 0.351): the 198,760 gil in the open retainer's purse
   became the starting point, and the withdrawal that followed was only a transfer.
2. The one-second pairs: when another retainer is opened, the game makes it the active one before its purse (`InventoryType.RetainerGil`)
   holds its gil; for one reading `RetainerPurse.Gil` gives that purse's figure in place of the list's, so the total falls, then comes back
   the next second. A fall is a withdrawal (transfer) and the rise above the deposits a sale (X-19, 0.769).

## The correction (0.1604, `Inventory/GilRecorder.cs`, pure and tested)

- The retainers' total starts from the last balance the journal recorded for them (`GilJournal.LastBalances`), once the journal is loaded:
  what rose meanwhile (a reload, a session) is their market sales. Nothing recorded yet: a starting point, as before (never a guessed zero).
  X-662 M1 (0.1606, review of 0.1605, R06): only when the character's first reading is also the journal's last balance of the character.
  A deposit made while Tensho was not watching (the game played without Dalamud, the plugin turned off) lowered the character's gil, a
  withdrawal raised it: then the rise of the retainers is not a proven sale, and their first reading is a starting point, as before 0.1604.
  The character's and the company chest's first readings stay starting points: no category can be told for a change made while Tensho was
  not watching.
- A new retainers' total counts only once read the same twice in a row (one second apart): a reading that does not hold is neither a
  withdrawal nor a sale.
- One way of writing the amounts: `TenshoCulture.Signed` (the language's culture: « -58.240 » came from Windows' culture beside « 320 834 »
  of Tensho's); the amounts' column titles on the right, above them (`UiComponents.HeadersRow`).

Not established offline: whether the retainers' list itself ever carries a retainer's sales before that retainer is opened (on 20/11 it
did not: the total was 0 until the first retainer opened). Either way each sale counts once: in the list or in the purse, the rise is the
same. The game test after a sale says it.
