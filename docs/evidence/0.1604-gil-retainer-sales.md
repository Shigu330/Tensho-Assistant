110,765 + 528 + 3,411 = 114,704 (the « Market » of the capture); 325,770 + 3,411 = 330,708 (the retainers' transfers); 325,770 + 999 +
528 = 327,297 (the character's). The gap of 3,411 = 471 + 999 + 999 + 471 + 471: the pairs one second apart, a fall then a rise of the
same amount (or the reverse), each counted as a transfer and as a sale.

## Causes, from the code and dalamud.log of 20/11

1. `dalamud.log`, 11:40:19: « Earlier instance 0.1600.0 loaded at 11:29:37 »: the plugin reloaded (a relay's new version) while the second
   retainer was open (11:29:00 « Reading retainer », the sale list read). `GilWatcher` took its first reading after the load as a starting
   point, never a change (by design since 0.351): the 215,005 gil in the open retainer's purse became the starting point, and the
   withdrawal that followed was only a transfer.
2. The one-second pairs: when another retainer is opened, the game makes it the active one before its purse (`InventoryType.RetainerGil`)
   holds its gil; for one reading `RetainerPurse.Gil` gives that purse's figure in place of the list's, so the total falls, then comes back
   the next second. A fall is a withdrawal (transfer) and the rise above the deposits a sale (X-19, 0.769).

## The correction (0.1604, `Inventory/GilRecorder.cs`, pure and tested)

- The retainers' total starts from the last balance the journal recorded for them (`GilJournal.LastBalances`), once the journal is loaded:
  what rose meanwhile (a reload, a session) is their market sales. Nothing recorded yet: a starting point, as before (never a guessed zero).
  The character's and the company chest's first readings stay starting points: no category can be told for a change made while Tensho was
  not watching.
- A new retainers' total counts only once read the same twice in a row (one second apart): a reading that does not hold is neither a
  withdrawal nor a sale.
- One way of writing the amounts: `TenshoCulture.Signed` (the language's culture: « -62.360 » came from Windows' culture beside « 327 297 »
  of Tensho's); the amounts' column titles on the right, above them (`UiComponents.HeadersRow`).

Not established offline: whether the retainers' list itself ever carries a retainer's sales before that retainer is opened (on 20/11 it
did not: the total was 0 until the first retainer opened). Either way each sale counts once: in the list or in the purse, the rise is the
same. The game test after a sale says it.
