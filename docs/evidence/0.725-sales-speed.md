# Sales a day (0.725)

User report, 2026-11-07, « What can I craft? », market sort: nearly every line shows « ? sales a day », and the top of the list is made of items that hardly sell. Request: count the units sold a day, so that the tool can be trusted.

## Measured, Universalis, 2026-11-07

Item 2753, Steel Sallet, on Moogle:

- `/api/v2/aggregated/Moogle/2753`: `dailySaleVelocity` has `dc` and `region`, no `world`. This is the « ? »: the aggregated answer leaves the world's speed out when there is no recent sale. Item 2340: `dailySaleVelocity` is empty.
- `/api/v2/Moogle/2753?listings=0&entries=8`: `nqSaleVelocity` 0.14285715, `hqSaleVelocity` 0, and the history shows one HQ sale at 83,000 two weeks earlier, the others near 19,900. One old sale put this item at the top of the list, at 83,000 each, for 31 pieces.
- `entries` changes the answer: with `entries=0` every speed is 0, wrongly; item 5057 on world 71 gives 40.4 with `entries=20`, and 50 with `entries=99`. Tensho asks for 200, and only these two fields come back. A numeric world works, one item or several.
- Windows differ: item 5057 on Moogle gives 158 by the aggregated answer and 49.9 by the item's answer. The item's answer is used: seven days by default, documented by Universalis as such.

## What changes

- Each price request now also asks the item's answer. If it fails, prices stay and the speed stays unknown, which is not zero.
- `MarketQuote.Sold`: HQ is not used for its price when HQ no longer sells (0 a day) and NQ does.
- Market sort: value = price × pieces that can sell in the chosen days (7 by default, 1 to 30), limited by what can be made. No sale: 0, so at the bottom of what is known. Unknown speed: after them.
- Copies on disk go from `universalis-aggregated-v3` to `v4`, so older ones are fetched again.

## Inferred, and limits

The result is a best case: other sellers share these sales, and the past week may not repeat. A sale at a much higher price than usual still counts in the last sale or the average, whichever is lower.
