# 0.1233 — Gold Saucer: GATE starts and the Jumbo Cactpot draw (X-223)

## Community schedule (read 2026-11-13)

FFXIV Console Games Wiki (GFDL-1.2-or-later), pages `GATEs` and `Jumbo_Cactpot`, facts only, rewritten:

- GATEs: one starts every 20 minutes of real time, « on the hour », « 20 minutes past » and « 40 minutes past »; which GATE is drawn at random.
- Jumbo Cactpot: drawn every Saturday (local time of the region). The page gives, per region, the time in two or three zones; every pair
  agrees on one universal time, which Tensho keeps:

| Region | Page | Universal time kept |
|---|---|---|
| Japan | 9 PM JST / 4 AM PST / 5 AM PDT | Saturday 12:00 UTC |
| North America | 6 PM PST / 7 PM PDT (Saturday) | Sunday 02:00 UTC |
| Europe | 7 PM GMT / 11 AM PST / 12 PM PDT | Saturday 19:00 UTC |
| Oceania | 9 AM GMT / 7 PM AEST / 8 PM AEDT | Saturday 09:00 UTC |
