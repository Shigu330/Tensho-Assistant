# GP alert after a teleport (0.656)

Report of the user, 2026-11-06: some time after a teleport, the GP alert rings although GP was already full before it.

## Reading of the code

`RegenTickReader` reset the alert when no player could be read, as during a change of zone. The next read became the starting point: any GP below the target at that moment, then a rise, rang the alert. `GpAlertTracker.Evaluate` rings when the previous read was below the target and this one reaches it.

## Change

The alert keeps what it knew across a change of zone, and does not read for 15 seconds after it. GP that truly rose during the teleport still rings, once.

## Not established

What GP or the maximum read after a teleport: no value was logged when this was reported, so the low read is inferred from the code, and 15 seconds is a choice. The capture now writes `Reading gp` around a change of zone, without the ordinary ticks, and `GP alert` when it rings, to compare.
