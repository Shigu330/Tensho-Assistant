# 0.1464 — Floors of a territory (X-462)

Probe: `PROBE_FLOORS=all` and `PROBE_FLOORS=820` of `tools/ShopCurrencyProbe` (French client sheets, game 2026.11.03, read on 2026-11-15).

## Why

On Eulmore (territory 820), Tensho's map showed, on each of its three maps, the icons (vendors, NPCs) of the other two. The game shows on each
map only the markers of its own range.

  TerritoryType 820 names map 498 as its map.

## What Tensho does (0.1464)

- `MapFloors.OtherFloor`: a map of the same frame with its own, different marker range is another floor; `MapCatalog.Equivalent` leaves it out.
- `MapFloors.MapOf`: a layout's placement (vendors and Triple Triad players absent from Level, bells and chests of X-386), recorded on the
  territory's map, takes the map of the smallest box holding it when the territory's boxes name two maps or more; in no box, on no map.
