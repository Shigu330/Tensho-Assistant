# Adjusted and fixed-quality recipes (0.666)

C6. Rules read in Craftimizer (`WorkingRobot/Craftimizer`, MIT, `Craftimizer/Utils/RecipeData.cs`, branch main, 2026-11-06), rewritten here:

- `MaxDurability = (MaxAdjustableJobLevel != 0 ? 80 : table durability) * DurabilityFactor / 100`.
- `MaxQuality` exists when `CanHq` or `RequiredQuality > 0`, which Tensho already did.
- `CollectableMetadataKey == 7`: a row of `WKSMissionToDoEvalutionRefin`, three thresholds in thousandths of the maximum quality.
- The level table of an adjusted recipe comes from `GathererCrafterLvAdjustTable` at the player's synced level.

XIVAPI v2, all rows, 2026-11-06:

| Rows | Finding |
|---|---|
| 768 with MaxAdjustableJobLevel | all at 100, with RecipeLevelTable 690; `GathererCrafterLvAdjustTable` 100 gives RecipeLevel 690 |
| 184 of them | CollectableMetadataKey 7, always collectable |
| 200 with RequiredQuality | CanHq false; 160 expert, 40 not; for example recipe 35127: RequiredQuality 13500 |

## Reading

As every adjusted recipe has level 100 and its own table is that level's, Tensho needs no table lookup: only durability changes. The refusal is lifted, and the page says what these recipes ask. The checks against the game's own numbers stay (`CraftLogObservation`: the maximum quality read in the log must match).

## Not established

- A character below level 100 in Cosmic Exploration: Craftimizer takes the player's synced level. Tensho simulates at level 100 and says so.
- Stats in Cosmic Exploration: not read. Tensho uses those it reads.
- What happens under RequiredQuality in game (failure or something else): only the number is shown, as a line, and it is not a target of the search.
- The three thresholds: their columns are unnamed in Lumina, and XIVAPI gives no field for them. The rule comes from Craftimizer only. Not compared in game. Not used as targets yet.
