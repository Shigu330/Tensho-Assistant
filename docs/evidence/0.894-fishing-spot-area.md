# 0.894 — fishing-spot areas (X-78) and the spearfishing node's base (X-79)

## Area of a fishing spot

The fishing log (« Carnet de pêche ») has its own map; the user's capture (2026-11-08, La Mer de Rubis, map 371, SizeFactor 100) shows three discovered spots
with their areas. FishingSpot rows (tools/XbmProbe, French): 160 Île de Bekko X 1680 Z 960 Radius 2000; 161 Okinoiwa X 1580 Z 360 Radius 1000;
162 Onokoro X 1050 Z 500 Radius 1000 (PlaceName 1029–1031).

Measured on the capture (by eye, the image was not kept as a file):

| | On the capture | Texture pixels | Screen / texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Okinoiwa → Onokoro (icons) | 273 px | 548 | 0.498 |
| Okinoiwa → Île de Bekko (icons) | 302 px | 608 | 0.497 |
| Okinoiwa, Onokoro circles (radius) | ≈ 75–78 px | ≈ 150–157 | Radius 1000 |
| Île de Bekko circle (radius) | ≈ 155–160 px | ≈ 310–320 | Radius 2000 |

So the area's radius is about 0.155 × FishingSpot.Radius texture pixels (the same space as X/Z, divided by the map's scale for world units). Approximate,
±5 %; on other map scales it is assumed to scale like X/Z (not checked). To check in game: the Tensho map's circles against the fishing log's.
