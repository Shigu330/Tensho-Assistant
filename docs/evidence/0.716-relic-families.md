## Where stages come from

The game has no list of them. The stage names and item levels were taken from the wiki's section headings (Anima, Eurekan, Resistance, Manderville, Phantom Weapons), then checked one by one against the `Item` sheet through XIVAPI, weapons only (`EquipSlotCategory` 1 or 13). Items come from the game; only the rules are in Tensho (`RelicBoard.Rules`).
