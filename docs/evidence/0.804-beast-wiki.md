# Where to tame a beast nearby (X-39, 0.804)

The game says where a beast is caught (zone or duty: docs/evidence/0.688-beast-places.md), not which enemy. The wiki's « Beasts » page (Master's Bestiary, read 2026-11-07) has a table: No., Name, Tamed from, Location, Min. ## The wiki's number is the XBMPet row

`tools/XbmProbe` (English, 2026-11-07): XBMPet rows 1 to 5 point to the Pet rows 47, 48, 49, 50, 52; the wiki's 1 to 5 are Cu Sith, Squirrel, Lamb, Pugil, Opo-opo, in that order, as the game's bestiary. Tensho takes the wiki's number as the beast's row.

## Beastmaster job

ClassJob row 43 is « beastmaster » (BST). Its job icon is 62100 + 43, as for the other jobs.
