the game said route 14 aboard (InstanceContentOceanFishing.CurrentRoute, « Ocean schedule check » warning of 0.880). Same arithmetic as GatherBuddy:

Research notes: `checkpoints/ocean-fishing-research-2026-11-08.md` (local).

## What 0.917 does
- Each departure seen aboard is saved (slot, route) in `Configuration.OceanSightings` and logged (« Ocean schedule sighting: slot, loop row, port,
  route, sightings, shifts still possible »).
- Per port, `OceanSchedule.State` keeps the loop shifts (0 to 143) under which every sighting of that port matches the loop column.
  No sighting, or several shifts including 0: community alignment kept, shown « not checked ». One shift left: « checked », that shift used.
  Shift 0 excluded: the port is « contradicted » and each departure shows the routes still possible, never a guessed one.
- The port of a route is read in the loop (IKDRouteTable: Route column = Limsa, Unknown0 = Kugane).
- The usual fishing help is off while the voyage director is read, or in a territory of the ocean fishing spots (FishingSpot.TerritoryType of
  every IKDSpot spot; probe output: 900 for the Limsa places).
