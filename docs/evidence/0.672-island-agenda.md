# Proposed agenda of the island's workshops (0.672)

N-10, second part. The design's search (best chains, then every set of four among the top chains) is reduced for a first version: the best chain for each workshop in turn, with the supply the previous chains used. `DP` over (hour, last craft): 25 × 81 × 81 steps per workshop at most. A test compares it with trying every chain, for one workshop.

## Community rules, written again

From WorkshopOptimizerPlugin (MIT, `Optimizer/Optimizer.cs`, `Optimizer/ItemSet.cs`, read 2026-11-03): an efficient craft shares a theme with the previous craft of its workshop and is another item; it makes 2 units and is worth twice; the first craft of a chain makes 1. The agenda's `Efficient` flag in the game (`EntryData.Efficient`, written by the capture) can confirm it.

## Known limits, said on the page

- Not the best agenda: workshops are filled one after the other.
- The groove stays as read: its rise during the cycle is not counted, so totals run low.
- Inside a chain, the search does not see its own effect on supply; the total, computed afterwards in the order crafts finish, does.
- Materials and rest days are not looked at. The player enters the agenda in the game: Tensho schedules nothing.
- The week's forecast (design, part 3) is not done.

Not validated in game. Open questions: `docs/evidence/0.671-island-workshop.md`.
