## Reading

- Tensho drew these pictures at the size of an icon: the white blotches.
- 0.631 drew the picture at its own size: too large on La Noscea (user capture), a wrong reading of a rough size check on Yok Tural.
- 0.632: measured on the game's crystals, 1 zone pixel is 0.42 map pixel, around the link's marker. Picture 1024 px for a zone of 2048 pixels, so drawn at 0.84 of its size.
