## Reading

- Tensho drew these pictures at the size of an icon: the white blotches.
- Size check on the game's capture: distances between zones give a scale of about 0.18, and the city's plan, about 370 px wide in the picture, covers about 65 px there: 370 x 0.18 = 67. So the picture is drawn at its own size in the map's pixels.
