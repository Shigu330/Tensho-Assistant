# 0.1599: the beast called for the combo highlight (X-655)

The user's report of 20/11 about 10:45 (test T-0.1558-2): in the Crucible of the Unbroken, with « Highlight the beast's combos » on, no « 1 »
or « 2 » on the hotbars, while the chat history shows the combo done (« You use Mistral Axe. » then « Intentional Combo: Moonstalker »,
20/11 about 10:38, and on 18/11).

## The cause, in the log (`dalamud.log`, 20/11, read only)

- 10:37:20 to 10:37:40: the game's « Cornes d'appel » window (`XBMActivePet`) is open while the team is chosen; at 10:37:34
  « Beastmaster battlehorns window: beast 5 (Trick 44943, value 2 True) » (the Opo-opo, `XBMPet` row 5, 44943 = 44933 + 2 × 5).
- 10:37:40 and after: the window is gone from the « Labyrinth reading » lines (10:37:42 « windows: », 10:37:50 « JobHudXBM0, JobHudXBM1,
  XBMContentsMainHUD »).
- 10:38:13: « in combat True », windows « JobHudXBM0, JobHudXBM1, XBMContentsMainHUD »: no `XBMActivePet`.

`BeastBoardReader.ReadActive` sets the beast to 0 when the window is not visible, and `DrawBeastComboHighlight` returned on a beast 0
without a word: in every fight the highlight stopped there. (`JobHudXBM0` and `JobHudXBM1` are the job gauge, not hotbars: the hotbars stay
`_ActionBar` to `_ActionBar09`.)

## The rule « 1 before, 2 after »: confirmed by the game

- `EventTutorialPage` 34 (French and English): « use your Cu Sith's Assault (its Trick), then Mistral Axe within seven seconds »: Intentional
  Combo: Moonstalker. 36: Moonstalker is Rampant → Durant or Eldritch → Volant; « your Cu Sith's Trick has a Rampant affinity, so when
  completed with Mistral Axe, it triggers a Moonstalker intentional combo ».
- `GuidePageString` 162 and `EventTutorialPage` 37: the second skill runs clockwise on the Compass, « whether a beastmaster or familiar strikes
  first »: the trainer's skill of the affinity before the Trick, used before it (« 1 »), or the one after, used after it (« 2 »)
  (docs/evidence/0.1530-beast-instinct.md).
- The user's fight: Opo-opo (Trick 44943 « Lapidation », Rampant), then Mistral Axe (44887, « grants you Durant Instinct ») → Moonstalker,
  as `BeastInstinct.Combos(5)` gives (« 2 » on Mistral Axe, « 1 » on Gale Axe, 44889).
- « Assaut » (Assault) is the game's word for the order given to the familiar: each beast's Trick is its own action (`Action` 44935 to 45033,
  odd rows, « Ordonne à votre bête d'exécuter … »), their icons 3906 to 3909 follow the affinity. No `Action` row is named « Assaut » in the
  beastmaster's range (probe of the `Action` sheet, 44860 to 45060, game 2026.11.03). Which action the hotbar's « Assaut » slot carries is not
  read yet: see below.

## The fix (0.1599)

- The beast called is read on the field first: the player's own battle NPCs (`IBattleNpc.OwnerId` = the player), the one whose name is a
  beast's name (`BeastCalled.Resolve`; two different beasts: none); the window only when no familiar is found. Not established yet: that the
  familiar is a battle NPC owned by the player and named as the beast. The reader says at each change « Beastmaster beast called: beast n
  (source); battlehorns window beast w; the player's battle NPCs: name/name row/base row/kind » : the in-game test tells.
- The highlight says in the log where it stops, or which bar and slot carry « 1 » and « 2 » (« Beast combo highlight: … »).
- While the combo's skills are looked for, the visible hotbars' slots are said when they change (« Hotbar slots read for the beast combo »:
  slot action, command type and id, shown type and id, icon, adjusted action), 30 lines a load at most: which action the « Assaut » slot gives.
- R03.1 changed on 20/11 (the user's choice, journal J174): the highlight works everywhere in PvE, no longer in the labyrinth only; PvP and
  ranked contents stay cut (`CompetitiveGate`). Option off by default; nothing is sent to the game (R03.3).
