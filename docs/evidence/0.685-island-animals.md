# Animals of the Island Sanctuary (0.685)

W-04, asked by the user on 2026-11-06.

## The game

`MJIAnimals`: 43 rows with `BNpcBase`, two `Reward` items, `Icon`, `Size`. No name, no place, no condition. The rows of value 1, 2 and 3 in its first byte number 16, 11 and 16, as the wiki's S, M and L do.

`MJIPastureHandler.MJIAnimals`, 20 slots of `MJIAnimal`: `SlotId`, `Nickname`, `BNPCNameId`, `FoodLevel`, `Mood`, `ManualLeavingsAvailable`, `UnderCare`. Field reads only; the functions that collect and feed are not called.

French words, `Addon`: « Pâturage » 15204, « Surnom » 15207, « Humeur » 15208, « Satiété » 15209, « Collecte » 15210, « Atelier » 14259. No word was found for the moods themselves, which the game may show as icons: the page gives the number the game holds.

## The wiki

The FFXIV Console Games Wiki, page Island Pasture, lists 43 animals, the same count as the sheet: 14 always available, 8 by time, 6 by weather, 15 by both. Its raw wikitext calls a template, so the rendered page is read. It is read on the player's click, under the wiki switch, kept in the cache and marked as community data; nothing from it is in Tensho's files. Times are Eorzea time.

Names, items and weathers are looked up in the game's English sheets and shown in the game's language; a word not found stays in English.
