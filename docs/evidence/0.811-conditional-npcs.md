# NPCs placed only under a condition (X-45, 0.811)

User report, 2026-11-07, with captures: on the Tensho map of Limsa Lominsa (the Aftcastle, the Upper Decks), vendor icons where the game shows none. 0.810 hid the NPCs of festivals not running; the report came back.

## Read

`tools/MapMaskProbe --lgb 128` (Limsa Lominsa Upper Decks, `planevent.lgb`), 2026-11-07: 202 layers with event NPCs.

- 136 have a `FestivalID` (for example « QST_Easter2021_Sea001 » festival 114, « QST_ChainaNewYear2016_Sea001 » festival 27): seasonal.
- 66 have none, among them « EVT_OP_ONLY_ENPC » (31 NPCs, the opening only), « QB_ClsRog301_001 », « QB_JobAst401_001 » and dozens of « QST_… » (quest steps).
- `planner.lgb` places no event NPC there.

The vendor positions taken from these layouts are only used for vendors without a Level row (`NpcVendorLoader`), so in Limsa every one of them came from a conditional layer.

## Use

Each position keeps its layer's condition: festival (shown while that festival runs), quest or opening (not shown), or always. Level rows stay « always ».

## Not established

- That every « QST_ », « QB_ », « EVT_OP » layer is conditional, and every other one permanent: read from Limsa's names only.
- Which of the icons on the capture were quest or opening NPCs: to check on the map after this version.
