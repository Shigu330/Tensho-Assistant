W-01, asked by the user on 2026-11-06 after reading the list of topics of a French site. Nothing comes from that site (`docs/project/REFERENCES.md`): the sheets and the structures below come from the game and from FFXIVClientStructs.

## Read on 2026-11-06

XbmProbe, French: `SatisfactionNpc` has 13 rows, row 0 empty, 12 clients. Each has an NPC, a required quest, a level, 6 deliveries a week and, by rank, `0, 90, 480, 780, 1080, 0` in `SatisfactionRequired`. `SatisfactionSupply` has the items by slot, `SatisfactionSupplyReward` the gil.

ApiProbe: `SatisfactionSupplyManager` has `Satisfaction`, `SatisfactionRanks` and `UsedAllowances`, 12 values each, `GetRemainingAllowances()` and, for the client whose window was opened, `CurrentNpc`, `CurrentSupplyRowId`, `CurrentSupplySubRowIds`.

## Inferred, to compare in the game

1. The three lists are indexed by client row minus one (12 values, 12 clients).
2. A rank is 1 to 5, and `SatisfactionRequired[rank]` is the points that rank asks for before the next one. The game's window shows the same two numbers if this is right.
3. `QuestRequired` is what unlocks the client.
