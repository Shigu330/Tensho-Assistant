# Custom delivery rewards (0.730)

W-01, left out in 0.675 because the currency codes of `SatisfactionSupplyReward` (2, 4, 6, 7) were not tied to items. Nothing comes from the French site of `docs/project/REFERENCES.md`.

## Read on 2026-11-07

XbmProbe and Lumina, game data: `SatisfactionNpcParams[rank].SupplyIndex` gives the `SatisfactionSupply` row of a rank; its subrows have `Slot`, `IsBonus` and `Reward`. Slot 1 is crafted, slots 2 and 3 gathered and fished. `SatisfactionSupplyReward` has 144 named rows, 12 by client: 5 ranks and the bonus, crafting and gathering.

## Why the codes are these scrips

`NpcVendorCatalog` already maps them from the game's scrip shops (`docs/evidence/0.95.0-shop-currency-probe.log`): 2 and 4 purple, 6 and 7 orange, crafters' then gatherers'. Item names read on XIVAPI: 33913 « Assignat mauve d'artisan », 41784 « Assignat orange d'artisan ». A second source agrees on every amount compared: the wiki page Custom Deliveries (consolegameswiki, read 2026-11-07):

| Rank, tier | Sheet: code 2, code 6, gil | Wiki: purple, orange, gil |
|---|---|---|
| 1, low | 94, 82, 670 | 94, 82, 670 |
| 1, high | 140, 116, 1000 | 140, 116, 1000 |
| 5, high | 180, 140, 1670 | 180, 140, 1670 |
| newest client, rank 1, low | 94, 97, 670 | 94, 97, 670 |
| rank 5, high, row 131 (eleventh client) | 180, 141, 1670 | Nitowikwe: 141 |

Bonus: the sheet gives `BonusMultiplier` 150 and the same base amounts; the wiki's bonus table is base × 1.5 rounded down: 201, 235, 270 purple; 1680, 2100, 2505 gil. Its orange row, 160, 186, 210, matches too.

## From the wiki only, said as such on the page

The second scrip is given only at the level cap, experience otherwise. `MinLevelForSecondReward` is 0 on every row, so the sheet does not confirm it.

## Inferred, to compare in the game

The objects of `SatisfactionNpcParams[rank].Item` (materia and food) are given on reaching that rank. The wiki only says that rank-ups bring rewards, not which ones.

## Not done

Experience by delivery: the wiki gives it as a share of the current level, the sheet has nothing to compute it from.
