# Masked Carnivale (0.683)

W-02, chosen by the user on 2026-11-06. Nothing comes from the French site whose list of topics suggested it (`docs/project/REFERENCES.md`).

Information only: stages, enemy names, times, done or not. The sheet also holds each enemy's weaknesses (`AOZContentBriefingBNpc`), which the page leaves out: that would be advice on a fight (DEC-009, DEC-033).

## Read on 2026-11-06

- `AOZContent`: 32 stages, rows 1 to 32, with `Order`, `StandardFinishTime`, `IdealFinishTime`, three acts and their fight type, gil, seal and tomestone rewards.
- `AOZArrangement`, with subrows: the enemies of an act. `AOZBoss`: 34 rows, one enemy each. Both lead to `AOZContentBriefingBNpc`, then `BNpcName`.
- `PlayerState.CompletedMaskedCarnivale`: 4 bytes, 32 bits, with its bit array.
- French words, `Addon`: « La Grande Mascarade » 8832, « Niveau » 12492, « Temps standard » 12454, « Temps idéal » 12455, « Réussi » 12443, « Non réussi » 12442.

## Inferred, to compare in the game

1. A stage's bit is its row minus one: 32 bits, rows 1 to 32, so a row used as it is would not fit.
2. Fight type 1 is an arrangement, and 2 and 3 are bosses: act ids of types 2 and 3 run up to 34, the size of `AOZBoss`, and the first stages read sensibly.
3. The times are seconds: stage 1 has 30 and 20, stage 25 has 525 and 435.
