# Spawn conditions of the S ranks (0.670)

N-12, first version, agreed by the user on 2026-11-06: conditions only, no counter. These conditions are not in the game's data.

## Sources (read 2026-11-06)

| Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| FFXIV Console Games Wiki, `Elite_Marks` (raw page) | GFDL-1.2-or-later | The conditions and the windows, rewritten |
| `srankguide.carrd.co` | none published | Certain or random, and the kinds of condition: facts only |
| GamerGuides, Endwalker S ranks | none published | Cross-check of the six Endwalker conditions: they agree |
| S Rank Assistant (`TheRedheadedWitch/SRankAssistant`, `Data/SRankData.cs`) | none | Read to cross-check the numbers, nothing copied |

Hunt Helper (MIT) has no conditions. `ffxivhunt.com` refused the fetch (403); the Floon guide redirected to a private address. Neither was read.

The rows come from `PlaceName`, `BNpcName`, `Item`, `Companion`, `Fate`, `Weather` and `Action` through XIVAPI v2, by exact English name, not from the plugins. Where a name has several rows: the mark's row is the one of its expansion (Ixtab 8890, Nunyunuwi 2960, Mindflayer 2955); Earth Sprite 113; the blue mage actions Northerlies 18297 and Self-destruct 11408; zones with use 1 (open world).

## Where the sources differ

| Mark | Difference | In Tensho |
|---|---|---|
| Gandarewa | The wiki gives no number; S Rank Assistant 50 of each | Both said in the text |
| Kirlirger | The carrd guide adds a time of day, without saying which | Said in the text |
| Orghana | Wiki: fly over after the FATE; guide: stand on the point when it ends | Both said in the text |

Certain or random comes from the carrd guide only; a mark it does not list shows neither. Its « guaranteed » assumes the hidden timer is ready.
