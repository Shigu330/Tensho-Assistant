# Where each region of « d.tex » goes (P11, 0.803)

Follows docs/evidence/0.766-map-discovery.md, which found the files but not where each piece of `{map}d.tex` goes. `tools/MapMaskProbe --png` (2026-11-07) wrote the textures as pictures.

## Reading

`{map}d.tex` (512×384) is a grid of 4×3 cells of 128 px. Each cell is the **whole map at 1/16** (2048 / 16 = 128). Region r is in cell r / 3, colour channel r % 3 (0 red, 1 green, 2 blue), at its own place in the map. Cell 0's red channel is the map's outline (inside dark).

Central Shroud (`f1f1/00`, DiscoveryFlag 16382 = regions 1 to 13): green and blue in cell 0 (regions 1, 2), red-green-blue in cells 1 to 3 (3 to 11), red and green in cell 4 (12, 13): the picture shows exactly that.

## Checked by counting (`cells.py`, threshold 128)

| Map | Regions | Outline pixels | Covered by the regions | Inside the outline | Overlaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heritage Found (`x6f2/00`, flag 2147483646) | 30 | 16 378 | 15 916 (97 %) | 15 910 | 10 |
| Central Shroud (`f1f1/00`, flag 16382) | 13 | 4 595 | 3 224 (70 %) | 3 088 | 4 |

Heritage Found's 30 regions tile the map like a puzzle, almost without overlap: this layout is the one the game uses. Central Shroud leaves 30 % of its outline outside any region, which the game would always show; to compare in game.

## State of each region

`MapDiscoveryManager` holds two tables of booleans: `MapsWithUpTo16Regions` (162) and `MapsWithUpTo32Regions` (48). The `Map` sheet gives the row (`DiscoveryIndex`: Central Shroud 0, Heritage Found 44) and the table: `DiscoveryArrayByte` true for Central Shroud (13 regions), false for Heritage Found (30): **inferred** to mean the 16 table, to check in game. Tensho reads these fields; it calls no game function.

Game patch 7.58 (game 2026.11.03, Dalamud 15.0.4.0, FFXIVClientStructs 7.57.0.9480; `tools/ApiProbe`, 2026-11-10): the structure grows from 4 132 to 4 164 bytes. `MapsWithUpTo16Regions` stays at offset 0 with 162 tables of 16; `MapsWithUpTo32Regions` stays at offset 2 592 and goes from 48 to 49 tables of 32; `ReportCooldown` follows at 4 160. Tensho reads both tables through the ClientStructs members and bounds the row by their length (no offset written by hand), so a rebuild against the new ClientStructs is enough (0.1037).

## Not established

- The meaning of `DiscoveryArrayByte`, inferred from two maps.
- That a region is undiscovered when its boolean is false (by its name, `Discoveries`).
- The game's own drawing of the edges; Tensho smooths the 1/16 mask between cells.
