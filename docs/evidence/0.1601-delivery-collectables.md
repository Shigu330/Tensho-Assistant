# 0.1601: the custom deliveries' collectables (X-657)

The user's report of 20/11 about 11:20 (captures kept out of the repository): miner level 87, a level 85 node, the collectable « Charbon »
(level 82): Tensho's Collectable panel said « no known turn-in threshold for this item in the game data », and « no plan ».

## What the game's window gave (`dalamud.log`, 20/11, developer mode, read only)

« Collectable window values »: value 2 is the item, value 1 the same plus 500 000:

- 11:08 to 11:10: 38821 « Charbon ».
- 11:14 to 11:15: 38822 « Ingrédient de plat de Voeburt ».
- 11:18: 36669 « Composants de matériel d'écriture »; 11:23: 36670 « Composants de fournitures scolaires ».

## Where the game keeps their thresholds (probes of the sheets, game 2026.11.03.0000.0000)

- Every sheet's number columns searched for those four items: they are in `GatheringItem` (713, 714, 697, 698), `SatisfactionSupply`
  (42.1 and 45.6: 38821; 43.1 and 45.7: 38822; 36.1, 40.5: 36669; 37.1, 40.6: 36670) and, for the last two, recipes and `WKSMissionRecipe`;
  never in `CollectablesShopItem`, the only sheet the panel's catalogue read since 0.409.
- `SatisfactionSupply` columns `CollectabilityLow`, `CollectabilityMid`, `CollectabilityHigh`: 240 / 450 / 600 for the four items.
- `SatisfactionNpc` 9 (Anden) gives the rows 42, 43 and 45 through its ranks' `SupplyIndex`.
- 324 of the 325 `SatisfactionSupply` lines have thresholds; 180 different items among them are in no `CollectablesShopItem` row.

So these items were never recognised by the panel (not a recent regression): the custom deliveries' collectables were not read at all.

## The fix (0.1601)

`CollectableReader` adds every `SatisfactionSupply` line with an item and thresholds to the catalogue (`CollectableRule.Delivery`), named by
its client; no scrips (the delivery's rewards are on the client's page), no level (the sheet gives none: « — » in the table). The panel then
shows the thresholds and makes its plan for them as for any collectable.
