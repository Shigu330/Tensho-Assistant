# Eureka's notorious monsters: weather and night (0.1241, X-205)

User request of 2026-11-12 (plugin comparison, K-35): when the notorious monsters of Eureka that need a weather or the night can next
appear, with an alarm. The game's files do not say which weather or time a notorious monster needs: that part is community knowledge,
cited here and shown as such in the page (DEC-044: community source marked, hidden by « Game data and what you observed only »).

## Source of the conditions

Eureka Helper, a Dalamud plugin, `snorux/EurekaHelper` on GitHub, licence Apache-2.0, commit `59fab9e911149cff992f1976ff90f73a6efd0463`
(2023-10-09), files `EurekaHelper/XIV/Zones/EurekaAnemos.cs`, `EurekaPagos.cs`, `EurekaPyros.cs`, `EurekaHydatos.cs`: each `EurekaFate` has
the FATE id, `SpawnRequiredWeather` (the monster), `SpawnByRequiredWeather` (the monster whose defeat calls it) and `SpawnByRequiredNight`.
Only facts were taken (which FATE needs which weather or the night); no code. Read on 2026-11-13. The 21 rows with a condition:

uses 19:00 to 06:00, inside both: a window shown is never outside the source's night.

## Checked against the game's files

`tools/ShopCurrencyProbe`, `PROBE_EUREKANM=1`, French client, 2026-11-13 (game 2026.11.03): every FATE id above has the English name of the
source's row (for example 1365 « Cassie and the Copycats » / « Défi : morbole de neige », 1403 « Heavens' Warg »), and each weather is one
of its zone's `WeatherRate` weathers:

- 732 Eurêka Anemos, rate 91: 2 Fair Skies, 6 Gales, 8 Showers, 15 Snow.
- 763 Eurêka Pagos, rate 94: 2 Fair Skies, 4 Fog, 14 Heat Waves, 15 Snow, 9 Thunder, 16 Blizzards.
- 795 Eurêka Pyros, rate 96: 2 Fair Skies, 14 Heat Waves, 9 Thunder, 16 Blizzards, 49 Umbral Wind, 15 Snow.
- 827 Eurêka Hydatos, rate 100: 2 Fair Skies, 8 Showers, 17 Gloom, 10 Thunderstorms, 15 Snow.

In the game, the self-check « Eureka notorious monsters » compares the table with the FATEs of each zone read by the plugin and with the
zone's weathers (`EurekaNotoriousBoard.Mismatches`).

## Inferred, to compare in the game

1. A monster « called » by another (the spawner) appears only after that monster is defeated: the window says when the spawner can be
   there, not when the notorious monster is up.
2. The window comes from the zone's forecast (the plugin's weather catalogue), like the fish and the vistas.
