## Reading

- `TempMapMarkers[0]` of the request holds the area: same icon, `Scale` 50 equals the radius of the event marker with objective 0.
- `StyleFlags` 1670 is the row of the Leve sheet here, and the request's title is the leve's name: this settles the open point of 0.655.
- The game has two states for this leve: one area of radius 50 (objective 0), and several of radius 35 (objective 530).

Tensho takes the request's area when it knows none for this leve on this map. What the game's list gives replaces it.
