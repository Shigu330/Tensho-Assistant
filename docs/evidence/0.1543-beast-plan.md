# Beast Labyrinth: the plan's ten and the plans kept on disk (0.1543, X-575)

User report of 2026-11-17 around 23:00, in the labyrinth on the 1st board with 0.1542 (two captures of the Tensho panel, kept outside the
repository): orange lines although the beasts were held. The guardian's three were Cu sith, Pampa, Acarus (1 of 3) while the orange line
said Écureuil, in the team, would hit Feu; fight 5 had Cu sith, Opo-opo, Pampa (0 of 3) while Salamandre, in the team, hits Eau; fight 4
called Piège-volant (Glace) but counted 0 of 2. With an empty team, the ten did not take every weakness of the board.

## Cause: a plan kept from before 0.1523

The plans file of the user (`BeastTeams.json` in the plugin's configuration folder, last written 17/11 12:21, read without change) holds
for the 1st board the ten 1, 5, 22, 20, 41, 12, 27, 2, 8, 16 and the trios 1, 22, 8 (guardian, 1 of 3), 20, 41, 2 (fight 4, 0 of 2) and
1, 5, 22 (fight 5, 0 of 3): exactly the captures. Those counts are those of the rules before 0.1523, when a magical beast had no element
(docs/evidence/0.1523-beast-weakness.md). The plan's key held only the beasts held and their ranks: the same beasts, the same key, so the
plan made by the old rules was shown again after 0.1523. The orange line (`BeastTeams.Missed`) is worked out at each change with today's
data, hence the contradiction between the line and the trio.

Fix: the key also holds a mark of the rules (`BeastTeams.PlanVersion`) and of the game's data the plan comes from (each beast's attack and
attributes, each enemy's weakness and stars, each board's size and limit; `BeastTeams.Mark`). Any older plan is made again once.

## What the game says about choosing

- DescriptionString 1731 (`aides.py cherche`): study the enemies' weaknesses to choose the beasts best suited to the fights.
- DescriptionString 1751: Force, physical damage dealt; Intelligence, magic damage dealt; Résistance physique and magique, damage taken;
  Vitalité, maximum HP: the order of the rules after the weaknesses (attack, then defence).
- DescriptionString 1734 and HowToPage 2595: the beasts are assigned to battlehorns (« cornes d'appel ») before the fight, and the Battlehorn
  action summons the assigned beast.
- Addon 17912 (`tools/XbmProbe "<sqpack>" French Addon` with `PROBE_MACRO=1`): « Niveau : <num(lnum1)> (synchronisation si le niveau est
  supérieur) … Rang de bête recommandé : <num(lnum3)> à <num(lnum4)> (synchronisation pour les bêtes dont le rang est supérieur) »: both
  numbers are given by the game's code, not by the text. « Symbiose » (the user's word, 17/11 23:50) is, in the help texts, the gear's
  spiritbond only; the user meant the synchronisation, which the text above states for ranks above the range.
- `XBMContent` columns 2 and 3 are 3/5, 8/10, 13/15, 18/20, 23/25 for the five boards; column 3 is the synchronisation rank (0.500).
  0.1545 (X-575 part 5) reads column 2 as the range's first number (`Board.RankRecommended`), the pair just after the team size (column 1),
  in the order the window shows them. To confirm in game: a note of 0.498 said the 1st board's screen showed « rang 1 à 5 »; if the window
  shows another first number, column 2 is not it. 0.1546 (X-576): the 2nd board's window shows « 6 à 10 »: column 2 is not it; the first
  number is the previous board's rank plus one (`docs/evidence/0.1546-beast-rank-range.md`). The warning « low for this board » never changes the choice (`BeastTeams.RankLow`; a rank
  not read is never said low).
- Not found in FFXIVClientStructs (`tools/ApiProbe`): no field of AgentXBMStageDetailList or AgentXBMPetParty holds the range; AgentXBMPetParty
  has GetSyncedRank and IsPetOverSyncedRank (functions, not called).
- The team in battle: the game's window XBMActivePet (« Cornes d'appel », `dalamud.log` 17/11 23:41 to 23:42) lists the beasts assigned to
  the battlehorns, one by one, each with its rank and HP.

## The ten with an empty team

`BeastTeams.Plan`: the beasts held are taken one by one, each time the one that adds the most enemies hit by their weakness over the whole
board (each fight's three at most), then the one strongest against the enemies it hits, then the best total attack; the places left go to
the beasts each fight would call, as before. A weakness still not hit is said under the ten: « none of your beasts hits it » or « the places
went to weaknesses of more enemies ». On the 1st board with the user's 37 beasts held, the ten cover the eight weaknesses (Foudre by Coblyn,
Gespenst or Adamankhélone; Feu by Écureuil, Spriggan or Draconide; Eau by Pugil, Mégalocrabe, Crabe or Salamandre) and every fight hits all
its enemies (test `BeastPlan1543Tests`).
