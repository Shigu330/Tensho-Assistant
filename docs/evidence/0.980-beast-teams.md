## Windows (developer reading, 2026-11-09)

Before entering, and on the board inside the labyrinth, the board is `XBMStageDetailList` and the team is `XBMPetParty` (texts: each beast's
attributes, name, then its rank). See docs/evidence/0.979-labyrinth-reading.md.

## Rules of the team (no formula from the game)

1. The team hits the weakness of as many enemies as possible (automatic attack of the enemy's weak type).
2. Attack: per enemy, the better of Strength / Physical resistance stars and Intelligence / Magical resistance stars.
3. Defence: Physical resistance weighted by the enemy's Strength stars, Magical resistance by its Intelligence stars, plus Vitality.

Every combination of 3 is compared. The 10: each fight's best 3 among the beasts held, the ones chosen in the most fights first, then the best
total attack. Ranks not read yet: the board's limit is assumed and said on the page.
