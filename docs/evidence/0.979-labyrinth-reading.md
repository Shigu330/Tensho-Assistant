Findings:
- The territory does not change between the board and the arena (1339 both): the territory cannot tell them apart.
- The game's « in combat » state is still false on arrival in the arena: waiting for it would be too late for the user's wish (« cut on arrival »).
- The board is exactly the time `XBMStageDetailList` (with `XBMPetParty`) is visible; it closes with the confirmation, before the loading to the arena.

included, whatever its name) has its numbers (Int, UInt, Bool by index, never a text) written to dalamud.log as « Labyrinth window <name>:
values=… », on change, 20 lines per window and visit. Since 0.1115 (X-137) the labyrinth is no longer cut; the rule below is the 0.979 one.

cut from the « Commencer le combat » confirmation on, on arrival in the arena, and during the fight. In doubt (window not found), cut.
