  « Rang de bête recommandé : 6 à 10 (synchronisation pour les bêtes dont le rang est supérieur) ».
- The 1st board's window showed « 1 à 5 » (note of 0.498, `docs/evidence/0.498-xbm-sheets.md`).

  numbers are given by the game's code, not by the text (`docs/evidence/0.1543-beast-plan.md`).

## What the plugin does

- The range's second number is column 3 (the two captures: 5 and 10).
- Column 2 is not the first number: 0.1545 read it and said 8 to 10 on the 2nd board, where the game says 6 to 10. Its meaning stays unknown.
- The first number is the highest synchronisation rank below the board's own, plus one (`BeastCatalog.RankFloor`): 1, 6, 11, 16, 21. It
  matches both captures (1st and 2nd board); the 3rd board and the two expert boards (11, 16, 21) follow the same rule and are to check in game.
- « Low for this board » is said only for a rank read below that number (`BeastTeams.RankLow`); the choice of the team never uses it.
