# Beastmaster bestiary: each beast's rank (0.1548, X-578)

## The request and the capture

The user, 18/11 around 00:15: « first find the rank of every beast: inside the labyrinth, the labyrinth's bestiary shows the ranks of every
beast one holds ». The user's capture of the « Bestiaire de dresseur » opened in the labyrinth (reported by the main session) shows for each
beast held « RANG 5 (8) » (the rank synchronised to the board, the beast's own rank in brackets), Exp 12/100, Force, Intelligence, Rés.
physique, Rés. magique, Vitalité, PV 608/608, Satiété 0/3.

## Where it is read

- The bestiary's beast page is the game window `XBMMonsterBookDetail`. The developer-mode probe (`BeastWindowProbe`, since 0.501) wrote its
  texts in `dalamud.log`: 126 lines from 17/11 10:09 to 18/11 00:08. Cu sith, 18/11 00:08:42: « 0/3; Satiété; 83; Rés. magique; 95;
  Intelligence; 125; Vitalité; 88; Rés. physique; 154; Force; 608/608; PV; 12/100; Exp; (8); 5; ; Rang; … Cu sith; N°1 »: each value before
  its label, then the name. Exp 12/100 and (8) 5 match the capture.
- Forms of the rank seen in the 126 lines: « Exp; (n); m; Rang » for a beast held (n its own rank, m ≤ n the rank shown), « Exp; Rang » for a
  beast not held (Pugil, 17/11 10:25: no numbers).
- The attributes the page shows are the sheet's at the synchronised rank: Cu sith (XBMPet 1, XBMPetParamGrow 42, rank 5): 154, 95, 88, 83,
  125 (Strength, Intelligence, Physical res., Magical res., Vitality), the page's Force 154, Intelligence 95, Rés. physique 88, Rés. magique
  83, Vitalité 125. Mouton (XBMPet 3, XBMPetParamGrow 21, rank 1): 50, 50, 91, 78, 113, its page the same. This confirms the place of each
  value.
- FFXIVClientStructs (`tools/ApiProbe`): AgentXBMMonsterNotebook holds the unlocked beasts and the growth sheet, not each beast's rank or
  experience; so the page is read.
- « N°1 », « N°3 », « N°4 » are the XBMPet rows 1 (Cu sith), 3 (Mouton), 4 (Pugil).

## What the plugin does

- `BeastBoardReader` reads the page's texts while it is open (in and out of the labyrinth, not during a cut), again only when they change;
  `BeastTeams.ParseBook` takes the only « (n) » text, the number after it, and the values at fixed places before it; the beast by its name.
  Anything out of shape gives nothing (R08); a page of a beast held that does not fit is said once in the log.
- `BeastTeams.WithBook`: the rank is the beast's own (a rank « at least n » becomes exact), never lowered under a rank known higher (X-561);
  the page (rank, synchronised rank, attributes, HP, experience) is kept in `BeastTeams.json` (`SavedPlans.Book`).
