## Rule kept (0.1572)

- The state line (chat type 57, no sender, in housing) is kept 1 s for the bed's menu that opens just after it at the same place; that bed
  keeps what the game said and when (`GardenBed.GameState`, `GameStateAt`).
- « Ripe » only when the game said so, or when the planting time plus the seed's growing time has passed and the game has not said since then
  that the crop was still growing. Said growing (or in need of care) at or after the expected time: « Unknown », with the game's words and
  their time under the mouse, until the game says ripe or the bed is harvested; withered: « Unknown » too. A harvest of such a bed is taken.

## The user's idea: the plant's picture changes, can it be read? (18/11, 00:45)

Probed read-only on 2026-11-20 (`tools/ApiProbe`, FFXIVClientStructs of Dalamud 15.0.4.1): `HousingObject`, `HousingFlowerPotObject`,
`HousingCombinedObject` expose only the game object's methods (position, draw object, timeline); `HousingFurniture` has Id, Position,
Rotation, Index and Stain; `AgentHousingPlant` holds the planting window's choices. No field gives a bed's growth stage, and which model is
which stage is written nowhere: reading the picture would be a guess. The state line above is the game's own statement, so it is used instead.
