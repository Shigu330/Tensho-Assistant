# 0.1529: the fights the game's board window shows (X-486)

The user's request of 16/11 (four captures at the Beast Labyrinth: the board layout at stage 1, « Formation de l'équipe », the enemy cards of
the Chevalier and the Évêque du plateau, the Tensho panel showing the whole board at once): on the team formation, a block of its own for
each fight, not everything at once.

## What is read

- `tools/ApiProbe` on `FFXIVClientStructs.FFXIV.Client.UI.Agent.AgentXBMStageDetailList` (17/11, Dalamud 15.0.4.0): `Entries` (1000
  `StageDetailEntry`: `EntryType`, `StageEventId`, `EventType`, `StageEventType`, `Name`, `Icon`, `BattleDetailId`, `BattleDetailIndex`,
  `ElementId`, the enemy's attributes, `ResistId`), `EntrySelection` (100 bytes), `ContentId`, `Mode`, `SelectedRank`, `ScrollToStageId`.
- The board's fights are the rows of `XBMContentBattle` (column 0: an `XBMBattleDetail` row, docs/evidence/0.500-xbm-columns.md). The
  entries' `BattleDetailId` is taken as that row (the field's name); the panel shows as « the fight now » the board's fights whose row the
  window lists, when the window lists some of them only. When it lists all of them, or none, the panel stays as before (the whole board).
- Fields only, no function of the game called; read four times a second while the board window is open (Beastmaster/BeastBoardReader).
- The log line « Beastmaster board window entries: N entries, fights …; selection …; stage to scroll to …, mode … » is written each time
  they change: still to read in game (does the window list one stage, or the whole board? what are the selection's indices?). Until then
  the selection is not used.

## Where the game's labyrinth shows them

`dalamud.old.log` of 16/11 10:33 (« Labyrinth reading », territory 1339): `XBMStageDetailList` and `XBMPetParty` open between the fights
inside the labyrinth, so the window's fights change with the stage.

## Actions

`XBMBattleDetailAction` column 0 is an `Action` row: its `AttackType` (1, 2, 3 physical; 5 magical with `Aspect`) gives the type of damage,
with the same `XBMElement` rows as a beast's automatic attack (0.1523-beast-weakness.md). The interruption and the effects of an action are
not read: no column of the sheets read is established for them (Lumina `Action`: `CastType`, `Cast100ms`, nothing named for an interruption).
