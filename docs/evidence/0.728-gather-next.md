# Next action of a node's plan (0.728)

User request, 2026-11-07: the gathering help shows the plan but not what to do next; if possible, mark the right action on the hotbars. Note: C23, the same idea for crafting through a hook, was set aside at the review of 2026-11-05. This request is for gathering and this version hooks nothing.

## How the step is worked out

No sheet ties these actions to a status: XIVAPI, `Action.StatusGainSelf` is 0 for all 24 of `GpActionCatalog`. So the step comes from what the panel already reads: GP and attempts. The plan's steps, those that last first, then attempt by attempt those that last one gather (`Bountiful`, `NextSuccess`). The steps done are those whose costs add up to the GP spent, the earliest possible.

The report's screenshot gives the case: 823 GP at the opening, 379 now, 3 attempts of 4, plan 50 + 100 + 200 + 4 × 100. Spent 444, not 450: 6 GP came back while the node was open, although `docs/evidence/0.650-gp-while-gathering.md` saw none in its window. Hence a tolerance of 30 GP, below the cheapest action, 50.

Two actions of the same cost can be confused: the earliest is assumed. What adds up to nothing says « no longer follows this plan ».

## GP given back by gathers (0.731)

User report, 2026-11-07: the plan was done with 53 GP left, boon at 90 %, and Pioneer's Gift I (50 GP, +10 %) was not offered. Same node: 823 GP at the opening, plan 800, six attempts. 823 − 800 = 23, and 53 after five gathers: 6 GP each, as the first screenshot (+6 after one) showed. Botanist level 100; another job or level may give another amount.

So the steps count 6 GP back per gather done, with a margin of 10. The plan made at the opening still does not count on them; once its steps are behind and attempts remain, the same optimizer is run again from the node as it is: GP now, attempts left, the slot's rates now, what was bought marked active. For the report: Pioneer's Gift I before the last gather.

## The hotbars

ApiProbe, FFXIVClientStructs: `AddonActionBarBase.ActionBarSlotVector`, `RaptureHotbarId`; `ActionBarSlot.ActionId`, `Icon`. `_ActionBar` to `_ActionBar09`, read on the framework thread every 6 frames while an action is wanted. The frame is drawn by ImGui over the slot, as on the Mini Cactpot board. `AddonActionBarBase.PulseActionBarSlot` exists and is not used: nothing in the game is called or hooked, and the action stays the player's.

## To compare in the game

That the frame sits on the slot at any HUD scale; that the steps follow a whole node. A gatherer's slot is expected to have the type Action: if not, « not on your hotbars » shows. Cross hotbars are not covered.
