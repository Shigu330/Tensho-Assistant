## The manuals and their cap

The manuals use `ItemAction` action 816 with one value in `Data[0]` (300, 301, 302, 303, 1751, 1752, 5329, 5330). That value is **not** the cap
and not an `ItemFood` row of experience: `ItemFood` 300 to 303 are meals (3 % experience, Critical Hit or Skill Speed, Vitality). No
column of `Item`, `ItemAction`, `ItemFood` or `Status` read here holds the cap. It is written only in the item's description:

| Item | English name | Cap in the description | Halved from level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4632 | Company-issue Engineering Manual | 250,000 points | 40 |
| 4633 | Company-issue Survival Manual | 250,000 points | 40 |
| 4634 | Company-issue Engineering Manual II | 500,000 points | 50 |
| 4635 | Company-issue Survival Manual II | 500,000 points | 50 |
| 12667 | Commercial Engineering Manual | 1,750,000 points | 70 |
| 12668 | Commercial Survival Manual | 1,750,000 points | 70 |
| 26554 | Revised Engineering Manual | 2,000,000 points | 80 |
| 26553 | Revised Survival Manual | 2,000,000 points | 80 |

Each says « a temporary 150% boost to experience points earned … (up to a maximum of N points) », duration 18 hours. The Squadron manuals
(14949, 14950) are other statuses (1081, 1082: +20 % for 120 minutes, no cap written).

## What the cap counts, and what Tensho shows (0.1760, X-221)

- The cap counts the **bonus points**. The game's help says so: each manual's description in French reads « Bonus maximum : 1 750 000 de
  points d'expérience » (Item 12667, column 8, game 2026.11.03, found in the index of the client's help texts), in
  English « a temporary 150% boost to experience points earned from synthesis (up to a maximum of 1,750,000 points) »: the boost is capped,
  not the points earned.
- The status's `Param` is not the points left: the user's reading of 15/11/2026 at about 8:35 pm (Commercial Engineering Manual used, one
  craft, `dalamud.log` « Reading statuses ») showed status 45, Param 70 before and after the craft, 64,774 s left. 70 is the level from which
  that manual halves its bonus (« +150% jusqu'au niveau 69, puis +75% », the user's screenshot of the same evening). The other three manuals
  of 150% halve it from 40, 50 and 80 (table above): Tensho takes their Param to be that level too; a Param it does not know shows no
  number.
- The game keeps the points left nowhere Tensho reads, so they are counted. The experience line of a job (LogMessage 589, the client's
  language; « Vous gagnez <kilo(lnum2)> (+<num(lnum3)>%) points d'expérience en <ClassJob lnum1> », the same three parameters in English,
  German and Japanese) is read by its parameters (Dalamud's `ILogMessage.TryGetIntParameter`, 0 to 2: job, points gained, bonus percent),
  as the gain sources since 0.1096. The percent is that of every bonus together; the manual's part of a line is taken as points gained ×
  manual's rate ÷ (100 + percent), its rate being 150 below the Param's level and 75 from it, never more than the percent shown. With no
  other bonus (percent = the manual's rate), this is exact; with others (a meal, rested experience), they are taken as added to it, so the
  page says « about ». A line whose job is not the one worn is not counted and is said once in the log (parameters that would have moved).
- Kept per character in the settings: the count goes on through a reload of the plugin when the status is the same (same Param, its time
  not started again) and the levels and experience of the jobs it boosts did not change in between; otherwise, or when Tensho did not see
  the status appear, the points left are « unknown » until the next manual. A new manual (its time started again) starts a new count.

### To confirm in game

- With a manual used while Tensho runs, the Gear page shows « Crafter's Grace: about N bonus points left » under the last crafter (or
  under the last gatherer), and N goes down by the manual's part of each line; the log says the first line read (« Grace: an experience
  line read »). When the game ends the status (cap reached), N should be close to 0.
