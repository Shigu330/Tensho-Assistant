# 0.1231 — Crafter's and Gatherer's Grace: what the game files say of the cap (X-221)

Read on 2026-11-13 from the installed game files (game 2026.11.03.0000.0000) with `PROBE_GRACE=1` of `tools/ShopCurrencyProbe`.

## The statuses

| Status | English | French | Description (English) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45 | Crafter's Grace | Grâce de l'artisan | Increased experience point gains as a Disciple of the Hand. |
| 46 | Gatherer's Grace | Grâce du récolteur | Increased experience point gains as a Disciple of the Land. |

## The manuals and their cap

The manuals use `ItemAction` action 816 with one value in `Data[0]` (300, 301, 302, 303, 1751, 1752, 5329, 5330). That value is **not** the cap
and not an `ItemFood` row of experience: `ItemFood` 300 to 303 are meals (3 % experience, Critical Hit or Skill Speed, Vitality). No
column of `Item`, `ItemAction`, `ItemFood` or `Status` read here holds the cap. It is written only in the item's description:

| Item | English name | Cap in the description | Halved from level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4632 | Company-issue Engineering Manual | 250,000 points | 40 |
| 4633 | Company-issue Survival Manual | 250,000 points | 40 |
| 4634 | Company-issue Engineering Manual II | 500,000 points | 50 |
| 4635 | Company-issue Survival Manual II | 500,000 points | 50 |
| 12667 | Commercial Engineering Manual | 1,750,000 points | 70 |
| 12668 | Commercial Survival Manual | 1,750,000 points | 70 |
| 26554 | Revised Engineering Manual | 2,000,000 points | 80 |
| 26553 | Revised Survival Manual | 2,000,000 points | 80 |

Each says « a temporary 150% boost to experience points earned … (up to a maximum of N points) », duration 18 hours. The Squadron manuals
(14949, 14950) are other statuses (1081, 1082: +20 % for 120 minutes, no cap written).

## What is not known yet

Whether the cap counts the bonus points or all the points earned, and whether the game keeps the points left in the status (its `Param`,
read by Dalamud's `StatusList`). Tensho does not show an « experience left » until a reading in game says which: since 0.1231 the
developer-mode reading (`Diagnostics/ReadingCapture.Dalamud.cs`, line « Reading statuses ») also runs for crafters, so a manual used in
developer mode logs the param and time left of statuses 45 and 46 at each change.
