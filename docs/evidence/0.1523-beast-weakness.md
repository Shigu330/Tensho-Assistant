# Beast Labyrinth: which beast hits which weakness (0.1523, X-553)

User report of 2026-11-17 around 10:45 (capture of the Tensho panel, kept outside the repository): « the help does not seem to make good
teams for weakness first »: on the 1st board the panel's trios hit no weakness in fight 2 (Archidémon, Foudre: 0 of 1), fight 4 (Glace: 0 of 2),
fight 5 (0 of 3), the guardian 1 of 3; and its text said the panel turns off when a fight starts (contrary to R03.2 and X-137).

## What the game says

- `aides.py cherche faiblesse` (help sheets, game 2026.11.03): DescriptionString row 1731, the labyrinth's help: boards have their own team
  size, and one should study the enemies' weaknesses to choose the beasts best suited.
- The board window (XBMStageDetailList, `dalamud.log` 17/11 10:13:45, developer probe) shows « Contondant ; Faiblesse ; Chevalier du plateau »:
  the same row as `XBMBattleDetail` column 8 for that enemy (7, Contondant). The weakness is a row of `XBMElement`:
  1 Feu, 2 Vent, 3 Terre, 4 Foudre, 5 Glace, 6 Eau, 7 Contondant, 8 Perforant, 9 Tranchant (`tools/XbmProbe "<sqpack>" French XBMElement`).
- The beast book (XBMMonsterBookDetail, same log, 10:09 to 10:10) shows each beast's automatic attack with the same words, beside
  « Attaque auto. »: Perforant, Contondant, Tranchant, and for magical beasts Feu, Terre, Eau, Vent, Glace, Foudre, Neutre.

## A beast's automatic attack (XBMPet column 5, an Action row)

`tools/XbmProbe "<sqpack>" French Action`: the two columns `AttackType` and `Aspect` (Lumina `Action.AttackType`, `Action.Aspect`):
physical attacks are AttackType 1, 2, 3 (slashing, piercing, blunt) with Aspect 7; magical attacks are AttackType 5 with an Aspect.
Before 0.1523 the catalog kept only the physical ones (rows 9, 8, 7) and gave every magical beast 0: it never hit Foudre, Glace, Eau…

Each Aspect value matched against the beast book: a book line read on 17/11 (its attributes at its rank and its « Attaque auto. » word)
names one XBMPet row through XBMPetParamGrow alone, whose action's Aspect is then read:

| Book word | Beast found by its attributes | Aspect | XBMElement row |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feu | XBMPet 2 (rank 1: 33, 110, 87, 87, 84) | 1 | 1 |
| Glace | XBMPet 27 (rank 2: 116, 116, 94, 81, 89) | 2 | 5 |
| Vent | XBMPet 40 (rank 1) | 3 | 2 |
| Terre | XBMPet 6, 11, 21 (aspects 2, 3, 4) and 28, 50 (4, 5): only 4 in both | 4 | 3 |
| Foudre | XBMPet 13 (shown at rank 5 on the 1st board) and 25 | 5 | 4 |
| Eau | XBMPet 4, 9, 15 | 6 | 6 |
| Neutre | (magical, unaspected) | 7 | none |

`BeastCatalog.ElementOfAspect` holds this table. With it, the user's ten of 17/11 (team window: Cu sith, Opo-opo, Pampa, Piège-volant,
Salamandre, Mandragore, Puk, Apkallu, Acarus, Mante) hit on the real 1st board 2, 0, 1, 2, 2 and 1 enemies (fights 1 to 5, guardian), instead
of 2, 0, 1, 0, 0, 1: Piège-volant (Glace) and Salamandre (Eau) now count. None of the ten has a Foudre, Vent or Feu attack: the panel now says
so for each weakness none of the three hits, and names the team's other beasts, then the beasts held, that would hit it (`BeastTeams.Missed`).

Not yet used: the enemies' vulnerabilities to effects (the team window's eleven effect names with a flag each, and `StageDetailEntry.ResistId`):
their meaning is not established (X-486, X-550).

Tests: `BeastWeakness1523Tests` (the 1st board's six fights from the sheets, the user's ten).
