# 0.1661: the Beast Labyrinth's team window writes each rank after a glyph (X-692)

## The report

The user's tests T-0.1526-1 and T-0.1526-2 of 20/11 (around 22:04 and 22:08, pictures on the tests page): in front of the game's
« Formation de l'équipe » window, Tensho's panel showed « Bêtes pour ce combat (équipe conseillée) » with Mantis, Opo-opo and Pampa, beasts the
player does not have, instead of « Votre équipe (lue dans le jeu) » (X-550, 0.1526); so the beasts down or low on HP were not said either.

## What the game shows (dalamud.log of 20/11, read byte by byte)

The developer mode's window reading wrote the team window's texts at 22:04:23.229 (« Beast window XBMPetParty: texts=… »), 169 texts. Each
beast is « …;Exp;608/608;PV;Chimère;<rank> » as in 0.1526, but the rank text is not a bare number: its bytes are `EE 80 B6` then `33`, the
character U+E036 (a private-use character: a glyph of the game's font) followed by « 3 ». The terminal shows nothing for the glyph, which is
why it was not seen in the log. The team of that window: Chimère 3, Mouton 1, Pugil 1, Dodo 3, Coblyn 1, Mégalocrabe 1, Puk 3, Crabe 1,
Gluant 4, Apkallu 4 (« Bêtes assignées 10/10 »), all at full HP. The names are the client's Pet sheet names (French, lower case in the sheet:
`XbmProbe … French Pet` of 21/11, rows 49 « mouton » to 85 « chimère »), matched without regard to case.

## The cause

`BeastTeams.ParseTeam` (X-550) and `BeastTeams.ParseRanks` (X-109) read the text after a beast's name with `int.TryParse`, which refuses
«  3 »: no beast was read, `State.Team` was empty, and the panel fell back to the advised plan. Nothing was said in the log. The board
window and the team window were both open (« Labyrinth reading: … windows: XBMPetParty, XBMStageDetailList », 22:04:35), so the window was
read at the right moment: only the rank text was not understood.

## What Tensho does now (0.1661)

- `BeastTeams.TryRank`: the number after the game's glyphs (private-use characters U+E000 to U+F8FF) and spaces, digits only; anything else is
  refused as before. Used by `ParseTeam` and `ParseRanks`.
- The reader says in the log how many beasts the team window gives when that changes (« Beastmaster team window read: N beasts, d down,
  l low. », never a name), and once per session when the window names beasts but none could be read (R08: « could not be read … the panel
  shows the advised beasts »).
- The test `BeastTeamGlyph1661Tests` replays the real window of 22:04:23 (glyphs included): the ten beasts, their ranks and HP; the three of
  each fight taken among them, the advised beasts the player lacks said with those in their place, a beast down left out.
