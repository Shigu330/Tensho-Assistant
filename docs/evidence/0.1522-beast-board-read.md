# Beast Labyrinth: which board the game is on, and the ranks its team window shows (0.1522, X-549)

User report of 2026-11-17 around 10:20 (capture of the game's « Formation de l'équipe » and of the Tensho panel, kept outside the repository):
the panel showed a board chosen by hand (« 2e plateau »), said « Rangs pas encore lus : le rang 10 est supposé » and « Les 12 à emmener »,
while the game was on the 1st board with a team of 10 (10/10) and showed each beast's rank.

## Cause

`MainWindow.DrawBeastBoardPanel` read the board from the territory only while `CompetitiveGate.Cut` was on. Since X-137 (0.1115) the labyrinth
is not cut (R03.2), so in the labyrinth the panel always used the page's list (here left on the 2nd board: team size 12, rank limit 10).

`tools/XbmProbe "<sqpack>" French XBMContent` and `ContentFinderCondition` (columns: row, key, territory, …, level sync, item level):

```
            1091|f1x4|1342|1|50|50
            1092|f1x5|1343|1|50|50
```

- XBMContent column 1 is the team size (10 for the 1st board: the game showed 10/10), column 3 the rank limit (5 for the 1st board).
- Each board has its own territory (1339 to 1343); `dalamud.log` of 17/11: « Labyrinth board panel on: territory 1339 » at the 1st board.
  The territory therefore names the board in the labyrinth (`BeastTeams.Current`, source Labyrinth).
- Before entering, the levels (30, 40, 50, 50, 50) do not tell boards 3 to 5 apart; the board is read from the game's windows instead.

## The board of the game's windows (FFXIVClientStructs of Dalamud 15.0.4)

`tools/ApiProbe` on `AgentXBMStageDetailList` (agent 502: `ContentId`, `Mode`, `Entries` of `StageDetailEntry` with `BattleDetailId`,
`ElementId`, the enemy's attributes, `EntrySelection`) and `AgentXBMPetParty` (agent 501: `ContentId`, `SelectedPets`, `CandidatePets`,
`PetCurrentHealth`, `PetMaxHealth` (15 each), `PetParamGrowValues` with `Rank`). 0.1522 reads only the two `ContentId` fields (no call),
while the board window is open; `BeastTeams.Current` takes either an XBMContent row (1 to 5) or its ContentFinderCondition row (1088 to 1092),
the two ranges apart. The reader writes « Beastmaster board read in the game's windows: content N … » when it changes: the next session in
game shows which of the two the game gives (still to read; until then a number of no board leaves the page's list, said on the panel).

## Ranks shown brought down to the board's limit

Same log, the team window's texts at 10:09 (1st board, limit 5): « Cu sith;5 », « Opo-opo;5 », … « Puk;3 », « Apkallu;4 ». The beast book
(XBMMonsterBookDetail) shows « (7);5 » for the same beasts, and the board's result (XBMResult, rank before, indexes 121 to 130) said 7:
the team window shows a rank brought down to the board's limit. A rank read at the limit is therefore only a floor
(`BeastTeams.MergeRanks`): it never replaces a higher rank known, and it is « known » only for a board whose limit is not above it.
The result window's ranks are the beasts' own and clear the floor.

Tests: `BeastBoardRead1522Tests` (the team window's real texts; the five boards' team sizes and limits).

## Read in game (20/11, relay 141)

`dalamud.log` of 20/11, outside the labyrinth with the 1st board's layout open: « Beastmaster board read in the game's windows: content 1
(board window 1, team window 1) ». Both agents give the **XBMContent row** (1), not its ContentFinderCondition row (1088): the first branch of
`BeastTeams.Current` is the one the game uses; the second stays as a harmless fallback (the two ranges are apart). Inside, the territory
1339 gave the 1st board, and the team window showed ten beasts with their ranks (« Cu sith;5 », « Écureuil;4 », « Opo-opo;5 »…), as the
tests replay. Still to see in game: a second board (T-0.1522-2).
