## What Tensho does with it (0.1719, X-225)

- `FreeCompany/AetherialWheelReader.Dalamud.cs` reads the window once a second while it is open, checks this layout at each reading
  (no more than 6 slots, each item a wheel of the sheet, primed or not, each charge from 0 to 100: else nothing is read, said once) and keeps
  each wheel per house (`HousingManager.GetCurrentHouseId`).
- When it is primed (`FreeCompany/AetherialWheels.cs`): seen being set (its slot read empty in the same opening of the window less than 15
  seconds before, now at 1 % at most), at the set time plus the sheet's hours; otherwise from its charge p: between (99 − p) % and
  (101 − p) % of the hours from the reading (the percent shown is taken within one percent of the time gone by, either way), the bounds
  narrowed at each new percent read. A primed item, or 100 %, is primed.
- The Reminders page has a row « Aetherial wheels » (each wheel: primed, primed at, or primed between two times), and the automatic alarms
  (the common setting, on by default) ring at the latest time each wheel is primed; a wheel taken off loses its alarm.
